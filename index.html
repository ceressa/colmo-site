Colmo is a number puzzle you can play with one hand and no hurry. You pour numbers into jars until each one is exactly full. 120 levels, an endless mode, and the small satisfaction of every jar filled exactly.

Free. Plays offline.

How to play

  1. Fill each jar exactly

    Every jar has a capacity. Pour the numbers you are dealt until it is exactly full: a full jar bursts and goes to the shelf. A number that would overflow is refused.

  2. Starred jars are the goal

    Burst the starred jars to win the level. Some jars have their own rules: a narrow neck only takes small numbers, and a wax-sealed jar stays shut until a starred jar next to it bursts.

  3. Three jokers, no clock

    Hold keeps the number in hand for later, Undo takes back your last move, Swap trades the number in hand for a new one. No level is timed. Finish with numbers to spare for more stars.
